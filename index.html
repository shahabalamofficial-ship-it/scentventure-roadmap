🛒 Sourcing Guide - Deira Markets

📍Murshid Bazar

Best for: Perfume oils, concentrates, dupes

  • Wholesale perfume & cologne market
  • Located in Bani Yas area
  • Multiple shops with competitive prices
  • Ask for "French oils" or "inspired by"
  • Best time: Evening (6-10 PM)

📍Deira Souq

Best for: Empty bottles, sprays, packaging

  • Al Souq Al Kabeer Street
  • Shaikh Saeed Perfumes area
  • 10ml, 30ml, 50ml, 100ml bottles
  • Caps, pumps, labels available
  • Negotiate for bulk discounts

📍Port Saeed Road

Best for: Perfumer alcohol, bulk supplies

  • Specialized chemical suppliers
  • Ask for "perfume grade alcohol"
  • Bulk discounts on 5L+ purchases
  • Gloves, syringes, beakers also available
  • Bring your own containers

🧪 Formulation & Mixing Guide

Basic Ratios for Long-Lasting Perfume

Type Oil % Alcohol % Longevity Best For
Eau de Toilette (EDT) 8-12% 88-92% 3-4 hours Daily wear, office
Eau de Parfum (EDP) 15-20% 80-85% 5-7 hours RECOMMENDED - Best value
Extrait/Parfum 20-30% 70-80% 8+ hours Premium, special occasions

📐 Calculation Examples

For 50ml EDP (18% oil concentration):
Oil = 50 × 0.18 = 9 ml
Alcohol = 50 - 9 = 41 ml
For 10ml Travel Size (18% oil concentration):
Oil = 10 × 0.18 = 1.8 ml
Alcohol = 10 - 1.8 = 8.2 ml
For 30ml EDP (20% oil concentration):
Oil = 30 × 0.20 = 6 ml
Alcohol = 30 - 6 = 24 ml
